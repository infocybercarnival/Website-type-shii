01 / INTRODUCTION

EVERY SYSTEM
HAS AN ENTRY POINT.

CyberCarnival is SRM Ramapuram's flagship cybersecurity symposium — a full day where students, researchers, and industry operators break systems, defend them, and rebuild them better. From live capture-the-flag arenas to red team exercises and hands-on workshops, this is where offensive curiosity meets defensive discipline.

ONE DAY. SEVEN ARENAS. ZERO SANDBOXES.

02 / EVENTS

THE ARENAS

  • 01CAPTURE THE FLAGCOMPETITION→

    A live jeopardy-style CTF spanning web, crypto, forensics, reversing, and pwn. Teams race the clock — and each other.

  • 02BUG BOUNTYCOMPETITION→

    Hunt real vulnerabilities in a controlled target environment. Findings are scored on severity, impact, and report quality.

  • 03RED TEAM × BLUE TEAMLIVE EXERCISE→

    Attackers breach. Defenders detect and contain. A head-to-head adversarial simulation on shared infrastructure.

  • 04PAPER PRESENTATIONRESEARCH→

    Present original security research before a panel of academics and practitioners. Best papers earn recognition and awards.

  • 05CYBER CONCLAVEPANEL→

    Industry leaders and researchers in open conversation on the threats, tooling, and careers defining modern security.

  • 06TOOL EXPOEXHIBITION→

    Student-built security tooling on display — scanners, honeypots, OSINT frameworks, and hardware implants.

  • 07WORKSHOPSHANDS-ON→

    Guided deep-dives with Supraja Technologies and Algorand. Bring a laptop, leave with a skillset.
